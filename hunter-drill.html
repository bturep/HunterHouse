Rick Hunter is an architect living in Victoria. He has studied Zen Buddhism for many years.

Performer bio · UVic Sonic Lab programme · HH-FRH-0004 · 1986

In 1985 the Victoria Zen Centre held a weekend retreat in the forest at Goward Road — an outdoor platform, work periods, a ten-minute kinhin train through the trees. Their history of it names the place Kendo Ric Hunter's house.

He drew a Zendo for the site twice, in 1977 and again in 1978. Neither was built.
